01 · While you’re away

Zappy stays home.

It keeps your cats company and safe,
notices what’s off,
and quietly sets it right.
So you can travel with peace of mind.

ZappyTo you
  • 03:14Luna knocked her water bowl over.Zappy set it upright and asked the sitter to refill it.
  • 09:40The water fountain stopped.Zappy restarted it. Water is flowing again.
  • 14:05Mochi got shut in the bedroom.Zappy found her. The sitter let her out at 15:00.
  • 22:30The heating went off. 16 °C.Zappy told you. It’s back on.

Good night. Luna and Mochi are fed, safe and asleep.

ZappyTo the sitter
  • 08:20Mochi ate half her breakfast.Worth a look when you visit.
  • 12:10Litter box: 4 visits since morning.More than usual. Please check on her.
  • 19:52Mochi just finished dinner.Time for her pill.
  • 20:05Sitter: pill given.Thanks. I’ll let Mochi’s family know.

The sitter sees what happened between visits.

03 · Meet Zappy

Zappy is home.

It notices.A tipped bowl, a cat that hasn’t eaten, a room that’s too cold.
It acts.It sets small things right, or calls your sitter.
It keeps you updated.As often as you like. And always when it matters.
