1. Who is responsible

Sushmita Singh, Germany. Email: founders@zappy-robotics.com.

2. What we collect and why

Founding households form. When you reserve your Zappy, we collect your first and last name, email address, your cat’s name, an optional photo of your cat, your city and postcode, and your optional answers (sitter contact, price interest). We use this only to contact you about the Zappy founding households programme and to plan the first trials. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give with the checkbox. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us. This does not affect processing before the withdrawal.

Emails to us. If you write to founders@zappy-robotics.com, we use your message only to reply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our interest in answering you).

3. Service providers we use

Tally (Tally BV, Belgium): runs our form and stores form answers and uploaded photos in the EU. Tally acts as our processor under a data processing agreement.

GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA): hosts this website and processes access data such as IP addresses. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Cloudflare (Cloudflare Inc., USA): delivers this website through its network and protects it against attacks. When you visit, Cloudflare processes access data such as IP addresses. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd., Ireland): only if you click “Allow” in the cookie bar, we load Meta’s pixel. It sets a cookie and tells Meta that you visited our site and whether you reserved a Zappy, so we can see which of our ads work. Meta may transfer data to Meta Platforms Inc. in the USA, which is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25 TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time by clearing this site’s cookies and local storage in your browser, after which we ask again.

Meta lead forms: if you sign up through a form inside one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, Meta collects your answers and passes them to us. We use them exactly as described for the founding households form above.

4. How long we keep your data

Until the founding households programme ends, or until you withdraw your consent, and no longer than 24 months. Then we delete it.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw your consent. Email founders@zappy-robotics.com.

6. Cookies

We only use one tracking cookie, Meta’s pixel, and only after you click “Allow”. If you click “No thanks”, we remember that choice in your browser’s local storage and load nothing from Meta.
