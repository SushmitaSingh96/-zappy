2. What we collect and why

Founding households form. When you reserve your Zappy, we collect your first and last name, email address, your cat’s name, an optional photo of your cat, your city and postcode, and your optional answers (sitter contact, price interest). Emails to us. If you write to founders@zappy-robotics.com, we use your message only to reply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our interest in answering you).
